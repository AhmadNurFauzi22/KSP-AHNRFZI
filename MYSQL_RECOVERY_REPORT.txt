Symptom: MySQL shutdown unexpectedly with error:
  D:\xampp\mysql\bin\mysqld.exe: unknown variable 'mysqlx=OFF'

is incompatible with MariaDB 10.4.32. This parameter is valid for MySQL 8.x
but causes MariaDB to abort on startup.

Affected file: D:\xampp\mysql\data\my.ini (and my_fixed.ini)
